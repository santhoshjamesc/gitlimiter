# The alert

When you hit a limit you get **a sound** and a popup with three buttons:

- **Open PR 🚀** starts the pull request.
- **See details 🖼️** shows which files are too big.
- **Raise limits 📈** gives you more room (+40%, then +30%, +20%, +10%).

Click **Test the alert** on the left to try it. Nothing is changed in your project.
