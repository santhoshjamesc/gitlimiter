# The status bar meter

Look at the **bottom-left corner** of VS Code.

| You see | It means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ PR size OK | Your branch is small. | Keep going. |
| 👀 Getting big | You are at 80% of a limit. | Start wrapping up. |
| 🚨 Open a PR! | A limit is reached. | Open a PR now. |

Hover over it to see the exact numbers. Click it for a summary.
